About Me

I am a Computer Science student at the University of Costa Rica with a keen interest in data science, artificial intelligence, and robotics. Beyond technology, I am also passionate about languages, literature, and the humanities, and I enjoy exploring the ways in which these fields intersect with computer science.

My academic interests include intelligent systems, computational analysis, and the relationship between humans and technology. I love seeing how computational methods can be applied to data of all sorts in order to gain insight and interpret the world we live in. Through my coursework, research, and personal projects, I seek to explore cross-disciplinary ideas and develop solutions that are both technically meaningful and human-centered.

I am always eager to learn, exchange ideas, and explore new perspectives.
